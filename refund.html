You may request a refund within 7 days of the purchase date shown on your receipt. Requests made after 7 days are considered at our discretion, but we generally approve them if the Software is faulty on your machine and we cannot fix it.

3. What happens next

We respond within 3 business days and, if the request is within the window, instruct Waffo.com Limited to refund the payment to your original payment method. 4. After a refund

When a refund is issued, the licence is cancelled. Please delete the Software and any copies of any wallpaper or sticker pack you also purchased from your devices. If you buy again later, you are welcome back — there is no blacklist.

5. Faulty product

If the Software does not work on your computer and we cannot solve it with you, you get a full refund regardless of the 7-day window. Please contact us first so we can try to help — most issues turn out to be an antivirus allow-list or a Windows compatibility setting.

6. What we cannot refund

  • Purchases older than 7 days where the Software works as described.
  • Requests where the Software has clearly been redistributed, resold, or shared publicly — these breach our Terms of Service.
  • Chargebacks filed without contacting us first. Please email us — it is faster for everyone.

This policy is in addition to, and does not replace, any statutory consumer rights you have in your country of residence. Where local law grants you a longer or stronger right of withdrawal, that law prevails.
