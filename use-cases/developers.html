What it does during your working hours

They sit under everything, not over it

Mochi and Snow stay transparent, borderless and at the bottom of the window stack, so they are never in front of the code you are reading. You see them when you look down, not when you are trying to click something.

Nothing is thrown at your windows

The classic open source desktop pets will pick up your windows and throw them across the screen. That behaviour is off here by design. A pet that can interrupt a drag operation is a pet you turn off within a day.

No network calls, no telemetry, no account

The desktop app makes no network calls at all. On a locked-down work machine that is the difference between a personal touch and a security conversation.

Long idle periods look right

Leave them alone and they settle into sleeping and blinking rather than looping a conspicuous animation. Four expressions per bunny are blended rather than cut, so a blink reads as a blink.

The honest drawbacks

  • They are decorative. They will not track your tasks, show a timer or read your CI status.
  • Two bunnies is the whole cast. If you want a specific licensed character, this is the wrong app — Desktop Mate sells those as DLC.
  • It is a Windows app. There is no macOS or Linux build.

If more than one of those is disqualifying, this is the wrong app for you, and the comparison page will point you at the right one.

Questions

Will a desktop pet slow my machine down while I compile?

It is hand-drawn 2D rather than 3D or Live2D, and it does not need a model pipeline, so it stays small. We are not going to quote a frame rate we have not measured on your hardware — if it is a problem on your machine, the 7-day refund exists for that.

Can I hide them while screen sharing?

Yes. The app can be quit and restarted with one click, and because it makes no network calls there is no account or session to lose when you do.

Do they capture my clicks?

The bunnies sit at the bottom of the window stack rather than floating in front of your work, and you drag them deliberately with the mouse rather than catching them by accident.

What is Mochi & Snow?

A Windows desktop pet: two hand-drawn lop-eared bunnies called Mochi and Snow that live on your desktop with 21 actions and 25 comic effects. One-time purchase, no subscription.
