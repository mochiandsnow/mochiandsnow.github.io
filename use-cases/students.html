Something small and quiet for a long study session

A library desk is a lonely place to work for four hours, and a phone is the worst possible answer. A pair of bunnies in the corner of the screen is a better one.

Written for people studying, revising, or working from a desk at home all day — including on a laptop that is not powerful.

Why it suits a study desk

It is light, so a cheap laptop can run it

Hand-drawn 2D art rather than 3D or Live2D models. Nothing needs to be loaded from a workshop, so there is no model pipeline sitting in memory behind your browser.

Nothing pops up in the middle of a page

They stay at the bottom of the window stack instead of floating over your notes, and they do not throw windows around. You will not lose a paragraph because a rabbit grabbed the window.

It works with the Wi-Fi off

The desktop app makes no network calls, so a dropped connection or a strict campus network changes nothing. Nothing needs to be signed into.

A small break that is not a phone

The two of them reacting to each other gives you a ten-second pause that does not end in an hour of scrolling. That is the whole pitch.

Before you buy

  • It costs $5.99. If that is not trivial for you, use the free in-browser demo instead, or the free open source Shimeji if you can install Java.
  • It is decoration, not a study tool. It will not block sites or track your hours.
  • Windows only, so it will not help on a school Mac or a Chromebook.

If more than one of those is disqualifying, this is the wrong app for you, and the comparison page will point you at the right one.

Questions

Is there a student discount?

No. The app is $5.99 once, with no subscription and no character DLC, and that is the whole price. A discount on that number would cost more to administer than it saves.

Will it run on an older or low-spec laptop?

It is 2D and loads no models, which is the part that usually hurts on a light machine. If you want to check before paying, the browser demo uses the same animation engine.

Can I turn it off during exams?

Yes, in one click. There is no account to sign out of and no streak to lose.

What is Mochi & Snow?

A Windows desktop pet: two hand-drawn lop-eared bunnies called Mochi and Snow that live on your desktop with 21 actions and 25 comic effects. One-time purchase, no subscription.

How much is it and what is included?

USD 5.99 once. Both bunnies, all 21 actions and 25 comic effects, six phone wallpapers, free updates, and a 7-day refund. There is no character DLC to buy later.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The browser demo is free, needs no signup, and runs the same animation engine as the Windows app.
